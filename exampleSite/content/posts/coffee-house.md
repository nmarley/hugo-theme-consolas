When I was around 20 or 21, I wanted to start a coffee shop.

I loved the coffee house vibe. Loved coffee, lattes. Who doesn't?

The hamburger shop across from the university had just shut down. It was a
perfect location. I wanted to put a coffee shop there so bad.

But I was a broke college student, and had no thought of raising funds.
Later, the university bought up the entire block and demolished everything
to put in a fine arts center.

And that is how I got radicalized against fine arts centers.

A one-week plan, by day.

Day 1: Go sign up at coinbase.com, add your bank account there.

Day 2: Login to Coinbase and buy $10 worth of Bitcoin.

Day 3: Download the Blue Wallet app. Write down the backup seed phrase on
paper, and put that paper in your passport.

Day 4: Send some Bitcoin to your phone app.

Day 5: Send some Bitcoin back to your Coinbase account.

Day 6: Security reminder. Don't give your seed phrase to anyone.

Day 7: Take a break! You're ahead of 95% of people who talk about Bitcoin
but don't do anything about it.

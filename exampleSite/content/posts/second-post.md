Everything I try and do, I try to reduce friction.

Why 3 clicks when it could be one? Why any clicks if not necessary?

I write comprehensive README files in my software repositories at work, in
order to reduce the friction that other developers will have onboarding or
learning a new system.

Momentum and consistency are key.

---
title: "Open, AI-enabled Tools are the Future"
date: 2026-04-06
tags: ["ai", "tools", "open-source"]
summary: "The tools which will persist will be model-agnostic."
---

Claude Code is great, but you know what would make it even better? Being
able to use any model that you want.

Ultimately the best thing for humanity is what will win out: free and open
tools which are able to be used by any model or model provider.

Open-source models will also win over closed source ones, because nobody
wants their AI to whisper their secrets to their nation-state overlords.
